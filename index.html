01 / About

Hands on, start to finish

I am a hands-on robotics engineer with experience in autonomous systems, mechatronics, and CAD, currently finishing a B.S. in Robotics and Mechatronics at Virginia Tech (in-major GPA 3.5).

The part I care about is the whole arc: leading a technical team through concept, design, prototyping, and deployment, and being there when the thing either works or does not. That has meant competition robots under hard size limits, a print farm that runs itself, and a printer chasing one micron of repeatability.
