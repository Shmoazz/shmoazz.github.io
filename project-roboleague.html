RoboLeague

2024 30 hour hackathon ESP32 + iOS

Overview

RoboLeague was a hackathon project: design, print, and program a small car running an ESP32, with a custom iOS app for control and scorekeeping. The idea came straight from Rocket League, except the pitch is a table and the cars are real, so people can play a physical soccer match with remote controlled cars and a live score.

My contributions

01 CAD, printing, and assembly

I modeled, printed, and assembled the entire car in under 30 hours. The design had three jobs at once: mount the electronics and route the wiring, stay compact and clean enough to look like a product, and go together fast, because at a hackathon the assembly time is part of the design. Simple DC motors and a motor controller drive it, with the ESP32 handling the wireless link and the control logic.

Technologies

  • ESP32 for wireless control
  • Custom iOS app, control and scorekeeping
  • 3D printing for rapid prototyping
  • DC motors and motor controller
  • CAD for the full car assembly

Results

What it did

Finished inside the hackathon window and working: a car you drive from the app, a score that keeps itself, and a game people actually stood around and played.
