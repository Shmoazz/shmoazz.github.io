5 DOF Robotic Arm

In progress 2025 Personal platform

Where it stands

No physical prototype yet. What exists is the start of a CAD model for a fully 3D printed 5 DOF arm, designed around ease of assembly, since I expect to take it apart and rebuild it often.

The point is practice: somewhere to write and break control and motion planning algorithms for serial arms, on hardware I own rather than in simulation.

Planned technologies

  • 3D printing for every structural component
  • Servo actuation at the joints
  • Raspberry Pi 5 as the controller
  • Forward and inverse kinematics
  • Control software still to be decided

Goals

Where it goes

Finished, it should work as a learning platform and a usable tool: motion planning, different control approaches, and eventually computer vision for object manipulation. The modular design lets one joint be upgraded without reprinting the whole arm.
